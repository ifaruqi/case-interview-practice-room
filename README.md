# Case interview practice room

A single-page practice tool for procurement and supply chain case interviews: 17 original cases (9 of them mirror the skills of a PrepLounge case and link to it), an interviewer guide, a candidate window to share on a video call, stage timers, scoring and history.

Open it: https://ifaruqi.github.io/case-interview-practice-room/

## How a remote session runs

1. The interviewer opens the page, picks a case and presses **Start as interviewer**.
2. They press **Open candidate window** and share that window in Google Meet (Present now, then A window).
3. Revealed exhibits, the current question and the timer update in the shared window. The interviewer view, with solutions, stays off-screen.
4. After saving feedback, the interviewer exports history (History, then Export) and sends the file. The candidate imports it to keep one record.

History is stored in the browser that ran the session. Nothing is sent to a server.

All companies and numbers are fictional. The page is built from case files in a separate repository; do not edit `index.html` by hand.
